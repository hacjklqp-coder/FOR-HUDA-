A letter for
Huda
✦

Huda, mujhe maaf kar do. Dil se keh raha hoon — yeh lafz bohot chote hain jo mujhe feel ho raha hai, lekin phir bhi keh raha hoon, kyunki tum sunne ki haqdar ho.

Mene tumse cigarettes ke baare mein jhut bola. Aur jo sabse zyada dard deta hai woh yeh hai ki tumhara trust toda — woh trust jo tumne 4 saal mein mere saath banaya.

Mene yeh chhupaaya kyunki main darta tha — tumhara reaction, tumhari takleef. Lekin chhupaana hi sabse badi galti thi. Tumhara sach sunne ka haq tha, aur mene woh haq ada nahi kiya.

Tum meri zindagi mein jo jagah rakhti ho — koi substitute nahi hai uski. Aur isliye yeh guilt itna gehra hai. Kyunki jo kuch bhi tumhara hai, woh mere liye sabse important hai.

Main promise nahi karunga — kyunki ek aur promise ki tumhe zaroorat nahi. Main actions se dikhaunga. Ek din, ek kadme. Tumhare saath honest rehna — har baat mein, hamesha.

✦   4 saal — yeh safar abhi khatam nahi hua
Tumhara
Tum meri duniya ho, Huda —
aur main tumhe khona nahi chahta. Kabhi bhi nahi.
